Hospital Bag Checklist: What to Pack for the Hospital

Complete hospital bag checklist for mom, baby, and birth partner. Know exactly what to pack and when to have your bag ready.

Packing your hospital bag is one of those exciting pregnancy milestones that means your baby is almost here! Having everything ready ahead of time will help you feel prepared and reduce stress when labor begins.

When to Pack Your Hospital Bag

Most experts recommend having your hospital bag packed by 36-37 weeks of pregnancy. If you're having twins or have a high-risk pregnancy, consider packing even earlier — around 32-34 weeks.

Hospital Bag Checklist for Mom

Documents & Essentials

  • ✅ Photo ID and insurance cards
  • ✅ Hospital registration paperwork
  • ✅ Birth plan (multiple copies)
  • ✅ Phone and charger (extra long cord!)
  • ✅ Camera if you want separate photos

Clothing & Comfort

  • ✅ Two comfortable nightgowns or robes (front-opening for nursing)
  • ✅ Nursing bra and breast pads
  • ✅ Comfortable going-home outfit (maternity size)
  • ✅ Warm socks and slippers
  • ✅ Pillow from home (use a colorful pillowcase so it doesn't get mixed up)

Toiletries

  • ✅ Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • ✅ Shampoo, conditioner, body wash
  • ✅ Deodorant
  • ✅ Lip balm (hospitals are dry!)
  • ✅ Hair ties and headband
  • ✅ Face wipes and dry shampoo
  • ✅ Glasses or contacts

Postpartum Recovery

  • ✅ Maternity pads (hospital provides, but bring your preferred brand)
  • ✅ Comfortable underwear (dark colored, high-waisted)
  • ✅ Nipple cream
  • ✅ Stool softener (ask your provider)

Hospital Bag Checklist for Baby

  • ✅ Going-home outfit (newborn and 0-3 month size)
  • ✅ Swaddle blanket
  • ✅ Hat and socks
  • ✅ Car seat (installed and inspected before due date!)
  • ✅ Diapers and wipes (hospital provides, but bring your preferred brand)

Hospital Bag Checklist for Birth Partner

  • ✅ Change of clothes (2-3 days worth)
  • ✅ Toiletries
  • ✅ Phone charger
  • ✅ Snacks and water bottle
  • ✅ Cash for vending machines and parking
  • ✅ Book or entertainment (labor can be long)
  • ✅ Comfortable shoes (you'll be on your feet)
  • ✅ List of people to notify after birth

💡 Pro Tip: Keep your car seat installed by 36 weeks. Many hospitals won't let you leave without seeing the car seat. Many fire stations will check installation for free!

Don't Forget!

  • Keep a list of important phone numbers (provider, hospital, pediatrician)
  • Pre-register at your hospital to save time
  • Tour the birthing facility beforehand
  • Pack a separate small bag for the delivery room (snacks, birth plan, comfort items)
