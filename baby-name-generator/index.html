Frequently Asked Questions

455|
456| How does the baby name generator work? 457|

Our quiz asks 5 questions about your preferences — gender, style, origin, starting letter, and name length. It then matches your answers against our database of over 1,000 names to suggest 5-10 personalized options.

458|
459|
460| Can I use the generator if I don't know the gender? 461|

Absolutely! Simply select "Surprise me" for the gender question and you'll receive a mix of both boy and girl names.

462|
463|
464| How many names will the quiz suggest? 465|

The quiz suggests between 5 and 10 names based on how well they match your preferences. If your criteria are very specific, fewer results may appear; broader preferences will show more options.

466|
467|
468| Where do I learn more about a suggested name? 469|

Each suggested name links to our Baby Names explorer where you can see its meaning, origin, and popularity ranking, plus browse similar names.
