Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At NeedsOfMoms, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data we handle and how we handle it.

Information We Collect

NeedsOfMoms is designed as a free informational resource for expecting parents. We do not collect any personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address through our website. You can browse our pregnancy calculators, baby name databases, and week-by-week guides completely anonymously without creating an account or providing any personal details.

The only instance where you might voluntarily share information is if you choose to contact us via email. In such cases, any information you provide in your correspondence is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is never stored in a database, shared with third parties, or used for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, which are small text files placed on your device when you visit a webpage. Cookies help us improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with our content. The types of cookies we use include essential cookies that are necessary for the website to function properly, and analytics cookies that help us understand visitor behavior patterns. You have the ability to control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, and you can set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being placed on your device. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some features on our website.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to help us understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about your visit, including the pages you view, the time you spend on each page, how you arrived at our site, and what you click on while visiting. This information is collected in an aggregated and anonymized form, meaning it cannot be used to identify you personally.

Google Analytics collects information such as your approximate geographic location (country or city level), browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and referring website. None of this information is personally identifiable. Google may process this data on servers located outside your country of residence. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on, which prevents data from being collected by Google Analytics on all websites you visit.

Third-Party Services

In addition to Google Analytics, our website may contain links to external websites and resources for your convenience. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies, and we have no control over their data collection practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external sites you visit through links on NeedsOfMoms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children's Privacy

NeedsOfMoms is intended for use by adults who are expecting or planning a pregnancy. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Our content is created for a general audience and does not target minors. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our records.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we collect through our website. However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect any data that may pass through our site, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Since we collect minimal data and no personal identifying information, the risk to your privacy from visiting our website is inherently low.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy. Your continued use of the website after any changes to this policy constitutes acceptance of those changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please do not hesitate to contact us. You can reach us by email at privacy@needsofmoms.com. We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days. Your privacy matters to us, and we are happy to address any concerns you may have about how your information is handled when using our pregnancy resources and tools.
