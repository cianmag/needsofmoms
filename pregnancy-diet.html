Pregnancy Diet: What to Eat and What to Avoid

Complete guide to eating well during pregnancy. Learn which foods to eat, which to avoid, essential nutrients, and healthy meal ideas for each trimester.

Eating well during pregnancy is one of the best things you can do for yourself and your baby. A healthy pregnancy diet provides the nutrients your baby needs to grow and develop, while keeping you energized and feeling your best.

Essential Nutrients During Pregnancy

Key nutrients: Folic acid (600 mcg), Iron (27 mg), Calcium (1000 mg), DHA (200-300 mg), Vitamin D (600 IU), Choline (450 mg)

Folic Acid

Critical for preventing neural tube defects. Found in leafy greens, fortified cereals, beans, and oranges. Start taking folic acid before conception if possible.

Iron

Supports increased blood volume and prevents anemia. Found in lean red meat, poultry, fish, beans, and spinach. Pair with vitamin C for better absorption.

Calcium

Builds your baby's bones and teeth. Found in dairy products, fortified plant milks, leafy greens, and almonds.

DHA (Omega-3)

Essential for brain and eye development. Found in fatty fish (salmon, sardines), walnuts, and DHA-fortified eggs.

Best Foods to Eat During Pregnancy

  • 🥬 Leafy greens — Spinach, kale, broccoli (folate, iron, calcium)
  • 🥚 Eggs — Complete protein, choline, vitamin D
  • 🐟 Salmon — DHA, protein, vitamin D (limit to 2-3 servings/week)
  • 🫐 Berries — Antioxidants, vitamin C, fiber
  • 🥜 Nuts and seeds — Healthy fats, protein, fiber
  • 🍠 Sweet potatoes — Vitamin A, fiber, potassium
  • 🥑 Avocados — Folate, potassium, healthy fats
  • 🥛 Greek yogurt — Calcium, protein, probiotics
  • 🫘 Legumes — Iron, folate, fiber, protein
  • 🌾 Whole grains — Fiber, B vitamins, iron

Foods to Avoid During Pregnancy

  • ❌ Raw or undercooked meat — Risk of toxoplasmosis
  • ❌ Raw eggs — Risk of salmonella
  • ❌ Unpasteurized dairy — Risk of listeria
  • ❌ High-mercury fish — Shark, swordfish, king mackerel, tilefish
  • ❌ Deli meats — Unless heated until steaming (listeria risk)
  • ❌ Alcohol — No amount is considered safe during pregnancy
  • ❌ Excess caffeine — Limit to 200mg per day (about one 12oz coffee)
  • ❌ Raw sprouts — Risk of bacterial contamination

Hydration During Pregnancy

Aim for at least 8-10 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration helps form amniotic fluid, produce extra blood volume, and prevent constipation and urinary tract infections.
