Naegele's Rule

296|

The most widely used method for calculating the estimated due date (EDD) is Naegele's rule. This standard obstetric formula calculates your due date as:

297|
298|
299| EDD = Last Menstrual Period (LMP) + 280 days (40 weeks) 300|
301|
302|

The rule can also be simplified as: add 7 days to the first day of your LMP, then subtract 3 months.

303|

Example: If your LMP was January 1, your estimated due date would be October 8.
