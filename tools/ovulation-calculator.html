Understanding Your Fertility Window

Knowledge is empowering. Learn how to identify your body's natural signals, like basal body temperature and cervical mucus changes, to better understand and predict your fertile window alongside using an ovulation tracker.

device_thermostat

Tracking Basal Body Temperature (BBT)

Your basal body temperature (BBT) is your resting body temperature. It often drops slightly right before ovulation and rises clearly by about 0.5 to 1 degree Fahrenheit afterward.

water

Monitoring Cervical Mucus Changes

As you approach your fertile window, hormonal shifts cause noticeable cervical mucus changes. You may notice an increase in clear, slippery, and stretchy cervical fluid.

psychology

Physical Symptoms of Ovulation

Beyond basal body temperature and cervical mucus, some women experience secondary physical signs of ovulation like mild pelvic pain or breast tenderness.
