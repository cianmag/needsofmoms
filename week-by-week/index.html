Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about week-by-week pregnancy tracking

When does pregnancy start being counted?

+

Pregnancy is counted from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Week 1 and 2 are before conception actually occurs. By week 4, the embryo is implanting and a pregnancy test can detect hCG.

How many weeks is a full-term pregnancy?

+

A full-term pregnancy is typically 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of your last menstrual period. However, babies born between 37 and 42 weeks are considered full-term.

What are the three trimesters of pregnancy?

+

First trimester: weeks 1-12 (months 1-3). Second trimester: weeks 13-27 (months 4-6). Third trimester: weeks 28-42 (months 7-9). Each trimester brings unique developments for baby and changes for mom.

When can I first feel my baby move?

+

Most moms first feel their baby move (called quickening) between weeks 16 and 25. First-time moms may not feel movement until closer to week 25, while experienced moms may notice it as early as week 13.
