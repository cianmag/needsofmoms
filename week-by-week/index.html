Frequently Asked Questions

When does the first trimester officially end? expand_more
The first trimester typically concludes at the end of Week 13. This period covers the first three months of pregnancy and is characterized by the most rapid phase of development for your baby.
How is gestational age calculated? expand_more
Gestational age is measured from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Since most people don't know the exact date of conception, this standardized method adds about two weeks to the baby's actual age from conception.
Is it normal to not feel pregnant yet in Week 4? expand_more
Absolutely. Many women don't experience noticeable symptoms until Week 6 or later. Every body is different, and the absence of symptoms is not necessarily an indication of an unhealthy pregnancy.
