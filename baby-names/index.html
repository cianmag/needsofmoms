Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about choosing a baby name

Choosing the perfect baby name involves considering factors like meaning, origin, how it sounds with your last name, family traditions, and current popularity. Our explorer lets you search by meaning and origin, filter by gender, and see popularity rankings to help narrow down your options.

Popular baby names change each year, but consistently top-ranked names include Olivia, Emma, Charlotte for girls, and Liam, Noah, Oliver for boys. Our database tracks popularity rankings so you can see which names are trending.

There's no right answer — it depends on your preference. Common names are familiar and easy to spell, while unique names help your child stand out. Many parents find a balance by choosing a classic name with a less common spelling or a name that's recognizable but not overly popular.

A name's origin refers to the cultural, linguistic, or geographic roots of the name. For example, Hebrew names like Noah have biblical roots, while Latin names like Olivia come from Roman tradition. Understanding origin can help you find names that connect to your heritage or simply appeal to you.
