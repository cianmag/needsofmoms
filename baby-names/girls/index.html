🌸 Girl Name Trends for 2025–2026

144|
145|

Baby girl naming trends are shifting toward nature-inspired names like Willow, Ivy, Aurora, and Luna. These names evoke beauty and serenity while feeling fresh and modern.

146|

Vintage revivals are also huge — names like Eleanor, Hazel, Violet, and Florence are making a strong comeback. These names carry the elegance of a bygone era while sounding perfectly at home today.

147|

Short, vowel-rich names continue to dominate: Mia, Ava, Zoe, and Ivy prove that sometimes less is more. Parents love their simplicity and international appeal.

148|

Mythology-inspired names like Athena, Freya, Luna, and Aurora connect your daughter to powerful stories and timeless meaning.
