Baby Registry Checklist: Everything You Need for Baby

The ultimate baby registry checklist. Know exactly what you need (and what you don't) for your newborn, organized by category with expert tips.

Creating a baby registry can feel overwhelming — there are so many products marketed to new parents! This checklist focuses on the essentials you'll actually need, organized by category so you can build a practical, no-nonsense registry.

When to Start Your Registry

Most parents start their registry around 12-16 weeks (end of first trimester / early second trimester). This gives you time to research products and allows friends and family to shop for your baby shower.

Must-Have Essentials

🍼 Feeding

  • ✅ Bottles (4-6 to start, even if breastfeeding — for pumped milk)
  • ✅ Bottle brush and drying rack
  • ✅ Breast pump (often covered by insurance)
  • ✅ Nursing pillow
  • ✅ Burp cloths (8-10 — you'll use more than you think!)
  • ✅ Breast milk storage bags
  • ✅ Formula (if not exclusively breastfeeding)

👶 Clothing

  • ✅ Onesies / bodysuits (6-8 in newborn and 0-3 month)
  • ✅ Sleepers / footie pajamas (4-6)
  • ✅ Socks and booties
  • ✅ Hats (2-3)
  • ✅ Swaddle blankets (3-4)
  • ✅ Going-home outfit

💡 Pro Tip: Don't buy too many newborn-size clothes! Babies grow fast, and many are already in 0-3 month size at birth. Ask for gift receipts!

🛏️ Sleep

  • ✅ Crib or bassinet (with firm, flat mattress)
  • ✅ Fitted crib sheets (2-3)
  • ✅ Waterproof mattress protector
  • ✅ Swaddle sacks or sleep sacks
  • ✅ White noise machine
  • ✅ Blackout curtains

🚗 Travel

  • ✅ Infant car seat (install before due date!)
  • ✅ Stroller (consider a travel system that pairs with car seat)
  • ✅ Diaper bag
  • ✅ Baby carrier or wrap

🛁 Bathing & Health

  • ✅ Baby bathtub
  • ✅ Baby wash and shampoo
  • ✅ Soft hooded towels (2-3)
  • ✅ Baby nail clippers or file
  • ✅ Digital thermometer
  • ✅ Nasal aspirator
  • ✅ Baby-safe laundry detergent

🎒 Diapering

  • ✅ Diapers (newborn size — start with 1-2 packs, babies grow fast!)
  • ✅ Wipes (fragrance-free for newborns)
  • ✅ Diaper cream / barrier cream
  • ✅ Changing pad
  • ✅ Diaper pail (or use regular trash with frequent emptying)

Nice-to-Have (But Not Essential)

  • Bottle warmer
  • Baby swing or bouncer
  • Diaper genie
  • Baby monitor with video
  • High chair (you won't need this until ~6 months)
  • Baby shoes (adorable but unnecessary for newborns!)

Registry Tips

  • Register at 2-3 stores — different price points and selections
  • Include price variety — items from $10-$200+ so everyone can contribute
  • Add a group gift option — for big-ticket items like cribs and strollers
  • Use the completion discount — most stores offer 10-15% off remaining items
  • Don't forget postpartum essentials — nursing pads, peri bottle, comfortable clothes for you
