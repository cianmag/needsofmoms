Baby Shower Ideas: Themes, Games, Food & Planning Guide

118|

A baby shower is a wonderful opportunity to celebrate the parents-to-be and shower them with love and gifts before their little one arrives. Whether you're the host or the guest of honor, this guide covers everything you need to plan the perfect baby shower — from creative themes and fun games to delicious food ideas and gift registry tips.

119| 120|

Popular Baby Shower Themes

121|

Choosing a theme sets the tone for the entire event. Here are some beloved options that work beautifully:

122| 123|

Twinkle Twinkle Little Star

124|

A dreamy celestial theme featuring stars, moons, and navy or gold accents. Decorate with star-shaped balloons, string lights, and moon-shaped cookies. This versatile theme works for any gender and can be elegant or playful depending on your color palette.

125| 126|

Woodland Animals

127|

Bring the forest to the party with adorable foxes, owls, deer, and bunnies. Use burlap, greenery, and earthy tones for centerpieces. Tree stump cake stands and acorn-shaped treats add charming details.

128| 129|

Boho Rainbow

130|

A modern, gender-neutral favorite featuring muted rainbows in terracotta, sage, and dusty rose. Macramé hangings, dried flowers, and earthy textures create a warm, trendy atmosphere.

131| 132|

Storybook Theme

133|

Celebrate the joy of reading by centering the shower around beloved children's books. Each table can feature a different classic like "Goodnight Moon," "The Very Hungry Caterpillar," or "Where the Wild Things Are." Guests can bring a book instead of a card with a message written inside.

134| 135|

Tea Party

136|

An elegant afternoon tea with tiered trays of finger sandwiches, scones, and pastries. Fine china, floral arrangements, and vintage touches make this a sophisticated choice. Serve a selection of herbal teas and a "mommy mocktail" bar.

137| 138|

Adventure Awaits

139|

Perfect for travel-loving parents. Decorate with globes, maps, hot air balloons, and suitcases. Signage can say "Oh the Places You'll Go" or "Adventure Awaits." This is another wonderful gender-neutral option.

140| 141|

Fun Baby Shower Games

142|

Games keep guests entertained and help break the ice, especially when family and friends from different circles are meeting for the first time.

143| 144|

Classic Games

145|
    146|
  • Baby Bingo: Guests fill in squares with predicted gifts. As the mom-to-be opens presents, they mark off squares. First to get a line wins!
    • 147|
  • Guess the Baby Food: Remove labels from jars of baby food and have guests taste and guess the flavor. Award a prize to the most accurate taster.
    • 148|
  • Diaper Raffle: Each guest who brings a pack of diapers gets entered into a raffle for a prize. This helps stock up the parents' diaper supply.
    • 149|
  • Measure the Belly: Guests cut string to what they think is the mom-to-be's belly circumference. The closest guess wins.
    • 150|
151| 152|

Modern Games

153|
    154|
  • Baby Predictions: Guests write predictions for the baby's birth date, weight, length, and first features. Seal them in an envelope to open after the birth.
    • 155|
  • Don't Say Baby: Each guest gets a pacifier necklace. If you catch someone saying "baby," you take their necklace. The person with the most necklaces at the end wins.
    • 156|
  • Wishes for Baby: Guests fill out cards with wishes and advice for the baby. Collect them in a keepsake box for the family to read later.
    • 157|
  • Baby Photo Match: Ask guests to bring their own baby photos. Display them and have everyone guess who's who.
    • 158|

Food Ideas

161|

Great food makes any celebration memorable. Here are crowd-pleasing ideas:

162| 163|

Savory Options

164|
    165|
  • Mini sliders or tea sandwiches (cucumber, chicken salad, smoked salmon)
    • 166|
  • Fruit and cheese platters with crackers
    • 167|
  • Veggie cups with individual hummus servings
    • 168|
  • Mini quiches or savory tartlets
    • 169|
  • Caprese skewers with cherry tomatoes, mozzarella, and basil
    • 170|
171| 172|

Sweet Treats

173|
    174|
  • Decorated sugar cookies (baby booties, rattles, onesies)
    • 175|
  • Cupcakes with themed toppers
    • 176|
  • Chocolate-dipped strawberries
    • 177|
  • A candy bar with the theme's color palette
    • 178|
  • Themed cake or a donut wall
    • 179|
180| 181|

Drinks

182|
    183|
  • Mocktail bar with fun names like "Mama's Little Helper" or "Baby on Board Bubbly"
    • 184|
  • Fruit-infused water station
    • 185|
  • Lemonade or punch in a themed dispenser
    • 186|
  • Herbal tea selection (safe for pregnancy: ginger, peppermint, rooibos)

Gift Registry Tips

190|

A well-curated registry makes gift-giving easy and ensures the parents receive what they truly need.

191|
    192|
  • Register at 1-2 stores — one big-box retailer (Target, Amazon) and one specialty store gives variety
    • 193|
  • Include a range of prices — from $15 pacifiers to $200+ big-ticket items like strollers
    • 194|
  • Focus on essentials first — car seat, stroller, crib mattress, diapers, bottles, swaddles
    • 195|
  • Don't forget postpartum items — nursing pads, nipple cream, recovery supplies for mom
    • 196|
  • Add group gift options — many registries allow multiple people to contribute toward expensive items
    • 197|
  • Complete it early — have the registry ready 4-6 weeks before the shower so invitations can reference it
    • 198|
199| 200|

Planning Timeline

201|

Use this timeline to stay organized and stress-free:

202|
    203|
  • 8-10 weeks before: Set the date and budget, choose a theme, book the venue
    • 204|
  • 6-8 weeks before: Create the guest list, send save-the-dates, set up the gift registry
    • 205|
  • 4-6 weeks before: Send formal invitations, plan the menu, order the cake
    • 206|
  • 2-4 weeks before: Buy decorations and game supplies, confirm RSVPs, plan the party layout
    • 207|
  • 1 week before: Prepare any make-ahead food, assemble favor bags, finalize the playlist
    • 208|
  • Day before: Set up the venue, arrange decorations, chill beverages
    • 209|
  • Day of: Set out food, put up final touches, and enjoy the celebration!
    • 210|
